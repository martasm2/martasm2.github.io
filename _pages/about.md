I am a **Computer Science Ph.D. Candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, advised by **Prof. Nancy Amato**, with an expected graduation in **May 2027**.

My research focuses on **efficient motion planning for dynamic environments and multi-robot systems**, with experience in collision detection, sampling-based planning, and learning-based methods.

During my Ph.D., I developed and extended **SPITE**, a dynamic roadmap framework for efficient replanning in changing environments. More recently, I have applied these ideas to multi-robot planning through **MR-SPITE**, which uses swept-volume approximations to reduce the cost of inter-robot conflict checking. My broader research has also explored **task-and-motion planning, vectorized collision detection, and space-time planning**. Across these projects, my work spans algorithm design, **high-performance C++ implementation**, and experimental evaluation.

I previously worked as an **Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon Robotics**, developing graph-based planning methods for autonomous mobile robots.

I am currently exploring full-time **Motion Planning Engineer** and **Applied Scientist** opportunities beginning in 2027.
